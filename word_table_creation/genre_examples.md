# Genre Examples

This list contains all genres, their purpose and example words. The words are all in German as the target audience is from germany.

## 0 - Alltag & Gegenstände
Very broad, good default backbone.
Examples:
- Schlüssel
- Rucksack
- Spiegel
- Kerze
- Leiter
- Tasse
- Batterie

## 1 - Essen & Trinken
Works in almost any group; easy associations.
Examples:
- Zimt
- Espresso
- Gurke
- Brezel
- Sushi
- Kakao
- Safran

## 2 - Natur & Tiere
Broad but not too abstract; great for mixed ages.
Examples:
- Delfin
- Fuchs
- Kaktee
- Tanne
- Gletscher
- Tulpe
(botanicals are fine here if hints are indirect)

## 3 - Orte & Reisen
Accessible, helps “theme nights.”
Examples:
- Hafen
- Pass
- Grenze
- Bahnhof
- Hütte
- Hotel
- Insel

## 4 - Kultur & Kunst
Covers music, literature, theater, museums; good for adult groups.
Examples:
- Ballett
- Geige
- Klavier
- Roman
- Galerie
- Bühne

## 5 - Sport & Freizeit
Keeps the base lively; avoid ultra-niche sports.
Examples:
- Schach
- Marathon
- Zelt
- Drachensteigen
- Stadion

## 6 - Technik & Digitales
Modern and widely relevant; avoid too much jargon.
Examples:
- Mikrofon
- Router
- Passwort
- Kompass (borderline but usable)
- Algorithmus (only if you can hint subtly)

## 7 - Wissenschaft & Medizin
Fun if you keep it mainstream; avoid lab-only terms.
Examples:
- Mikroskop
- Penicillin
- Gravitation
- Molekül (careful with hints)

## 8 - Geschichte & Mythos
Adds flavor; keep terms recognizable.
Examples:
- Labyrinth
- Ritter
- Pharao
- Mythos
- Legende

## 9 - Gesellschaft & Beruf
Relatable, but keep it light.
Examples:
- Bewerbung
- Chef
- Vertrag
- Kollegin
- Büro
- Meeting

## 10 - Haushalt & Wohnen
Highly accessible; pairs well with “Alltag.”
Examples:
- Staubsauger
- Herd
- Balkon
- Kissen
- Vorhang

## 11 - Wetter & Himmel
Great for indirect hinting, visuals, metaphors.
Examples:
- Regenbogen
- Gewitter
- Aurora/Polarlicht
- Nebel
- Eclipse/Sonnenfinsternis

## 12 - Gefühle & Abstraktes
Important for variety, but keep words “hintable.”
Examples:
- Flüstern
- Geduld
- Neid
- Hoffnung
- Chaos
- Echo

## 13 - Feste & Jahreszeiten
Seasonal bundles and family rounds.
Examples:
- Feuerwerk
- Kürbis
- Advent
- Karneval
- Frühling

## 14 - Höhere Mathematik
For students at university studying mathematics
Examples:
- Ring
- Körper
- Holomorphie
- Homomorphismus
- Teilerfremd
